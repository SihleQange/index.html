ABOUT QFS

Practical solutions for sustainable development.

QFS is focused on supporting sustainable development through practical interventions in agriculture, food systems and community livelihoods.

We bring together technical knowledge, programme implementation, stakeholder engagement and community-centred approaches to address development challenges.

Our work is grounded in the belief that sustainable solutions must create value for communities while strengthening resilience, livelihoods and local economic opportunities.

Our Vision

Stronger communities, sustainable food systems and inclusive economic opportunities built through practical and locally relevant solutions.
